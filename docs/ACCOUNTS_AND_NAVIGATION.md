# Creating logins in Trellis

Sign in with your instructor account, then open **Course settings → Enrollment**.

1. Under **Add people**, enter the person's email and select **Student**, **TA**, or **Instructor**.
2. Leave **Create a login when adding a new account** checked.
3. Enter a password, or leave it blank to generate one.
4. Click **Add person**. Use **Copy login details** to share the email/password with that person.

Trellis does not send an email automatically. Password details appear in the
confirmation panel; closing the panel or leaving the page clears them from the UI.
Passwords are stored as hashes on the server, not readable roster fields.

For someone already on the roster, use **Create login** if their account is
pending. Use **Reset password** for an existing login. Enter a new password in the
reset window, or leave it blank to generate one; the old password stops working
when you save. Only instructors can provision/reset logins.
Students and TAs cannot use these controls. Your own password cannot be reset
through this roster action.

CSV upload adds people to the roster. Pending accounts can then be activated with
the **Create login** button on their row. Normal student registration remains
disabled by default. The local instructor can add demo accounts.

## Navigation and saved place

Pages and activity selections have URLs. Browser Back/Forward, refresh, duplicate
tabs, and copied links restore the selected page. Course section cards also support
opening in a new tab. Unsaved changes and active exams retain their navigation guards.

Signing in at the main URL opens Home directly. **Pick up where you left off** uses
the learner's most recently viewed available activity, stored with their account.
Deleted/hidden activities and all programming quizzes (including their questions)
are excluded from resume suggestions for both students and staff.

## Restarting a staff test quiz

In student view, open a submitted programming quiz and choose **Reset my student-view
attempt**. Alternatively, use **Course settings → Programming quizzes → Reset my
test attempt**. Confirming restores your own quiz files, clears your test score,
and allows a fresh timer when you begin again. It does not reset any student's
attempt, saved work, or score. Historical submissions remain available to staff.
Students cannot use this action.
